Phase 2 — the main new direction: symmetry-aware growth (reconstruct Galebach's method). Galebach's images suggest he grew a seed to a constant radius of polygons, exploiting thetiling's rotational symmetry. Build a symmetry-first geometric grower: commit to a rotation center of order m∈{2,3,4,6} (and reflections), grow only the fundamental sector (≤ 1/m of the cell, up to ~1/12 with reflections) to the determining radius (Local Theorem — small in E²), and let the group close the corona. This builds only valid euclidean patches (no hyperbolic waste, no c^V full cell), is bounded-memory, and is periodic-by-construction (no aperiodic scatter). Enumerate over (rotation order, central configuration,sector growth); dedup and verify every result via DelaneySymbols.classifyClosedMap/minimalSymbol/canonicalKey against the n≤3 oracle then TilingReference.counts. Phase 3 — combine + residual. Use symmetry-growth for the symmetric majority; fall back to bounded-V (now chiral-correct) only for genuinely low-symmetry small-cell tilings.
